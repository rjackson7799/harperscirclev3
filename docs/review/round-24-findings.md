## Findings, most severe first

### F-1 — MAJOR — `hc.document_references` (and `hc.shares_for_member`'s LEFT-join branch) counts an object its caller holds at `hidden`: "counted, never named" is applied below `log`, where PRD §7.3/§7.6 say the object does not exist for that caller in any count
**Confidence.** High. Not contingent — driven on the live function.
**Where.** `supabase/migrations/20260829120004_record_reads.sql:266–273` — the SELECT over `refs r join lateral hc.object_label_at(v_ctx, r.otype, r.oid) x` emits one row per descendant with `case when x.level >= x.need then r.oid end`, `case when x.level >= x.need then x.label end`, `(x.level >= x.need)`; there is no floor on `x.level`. `hc.shares_for_member` `:363–371` does the same through a LEFT join with `coalesce(x.level >= x.need, false)`. Pinned by 069:18 (`'task:NULL:false:false,…'` for Priya) and 069:28.
**Claim under test.** M4 header `:44–53`: *"each at the CALLER's own level of the destination through that destination's OWN policy predicate … COUNTED, NEVER NAMED: object_type survives, object_id and label are suppressed TOGETHER, `visible` is explicit"*; packet: *"never wider than the RLS it stands in for"*; ADR-0032 D7: *"counted-never-named through each object's OWN policy predicate"*, citing TSD §3.5 and `hc.receipt_for`.
**What I found.** `visible = false` conflates two levels the model keeps apart. At `log`, existence is exactly what the level grants (TSD §3.5: *"presence and activity: that things exist"*), and emitting a typed, unnamed row is right. At `hidden`, PRD §7.3 says *"Nothing. The domain's existence is not implied by a count, an empty section or a disabled control"*, §7.6 says *"If any domain in the taint is `hidden` for them, the object does not exist for them … in any surface, in any count, in any export"* and *"Existence disclosure is a grant, never a derivation … reachable only by a member holding `log` or above on every domain in the taint"*, and AC-PERM-6 spells out *"invisible — title, due date, existence, and its contribution to every count"*. The read's gate is `documents_select`'s predicate on the DOCUMENT (`:245–250`), and descendants carry domains the document does not (a task adds `schedule`), so a summary reader of the document can hold a descendant at `hidden` — the fixture's own Priya. The `hc.receipt_for` precedent the header cites does not transfer: `receipt_for`'s gate is `view` over `hc.all_domains()` on the arrival (`20260824120005:102–109`), so its reader already clears every domain a destination can carry and an invisible destination is reachable only through unresolved lineage, deletion or a cap — RCP-01's cell says exactly that ("the honest bound on the filter"). `document_references` has no such dominance; it is gated at `summary` on one object and walks into domains the gate never checked. `hc.presence(subject)` — the one sanctioned route to existence — refuses the same object to the same caller, because it filters at `>= 'log'` (`20260815230009:259+`).
**Failure scenario.** Probe G (`probe-r1.sql`), Priya = family, `health: summary`, nothing on `schedule`; `d_med` (medical, `{health}`), `t_der` drafted from it (`{schedule,health}`):
```
 G1 priya document_references(d_med) | task:NULL:false
 G2 priya level on t_der via visible_at | hidden
 G3 priya tasks_select on t_der | 0
 G4 priya hc.presence(s1) includes t_der? | 0
```
Priya, who holds `schedule` at `hidden`, is told that a task was drafted from the discharge summary — a schedule-domain object `tasks_select` does not return to her and `hc.presence` does not count for her. The People & roles sentence 7C will render for her says she has nothing on the schedule; the Documents detail 7C will render from this read says "1 task references this document". That is the A.4 existence oracle §7.6 names ("Counts are content at the margin"). The same emission reaches a caller holding a domain at `hidden` for a timeline event or an episode under the document; and `shares_for_member` counts a coordinator-listed member's share on an object the coordinator holds at `hidden` (a coordinator without manage×5 — 069's Kim shape — on a subject where the object's taint reaches her `hidden` domain).
**Why the tests miss it.** 069:18 asserts the leak as its expected value (`task:NULL:false:false`), titled *"both are reported as existing and neither is named nor handed to her"*. No fixture member holds a domain at `log`, so nothing in 069 distinguishes the `log` case (correct to count) from the `hidden` case (forbidden to count); the test proves the suppression of name and handle and never asks whether the row should exist.
**What would close it.** No DDL: a floor in the WHERE of `document_references` (`x.level >= 'log'`) and in `shares_for_member`'s branch for callers other than the share-holder herself (a share-holder's own share is one she was told of at grant time — the owner may rule that her own rows stay counted), with 069:18/:20/:28 re-pinned and one new assertion each way (a `log` holder counts, a `hidden` holder does not). The coverage cell DOC-02's *"references and shares count-never-name"* and DOC-04's *"a share never reaches a derived object at the read (069:20)"* stay true under the floor — 069:20's Ruth holds `documents` summary and a share, and her level on each descendant is `hidden`, so under the floor 069:20 returns zero rows, which is the stronger AC-PERM-10.

### F-2 — MAJOR — A coordinator's KEPT share survives exactly one call: `created_by_assignment_of` identifies the task, not the assignment, so the next `unassign_task` or reassign of that task — by anyone holding manage — revokes it
**Confidence.** High. Driven on the live function, three ways.
**Where.** `20260829120001:556–562` (the unassign loop: `where sh.created_by_assignment_of = p_task and sh.revoked_at is null and not (sh.id = any (v_keep))`) and `:332–336` (the reassign loop inside `assign_task`: the same predicate with no keep list) — neither filters on the share's `member_id` or on the former holder; `object_shares` carries no assignment-instance key (`20260815230003:31–46`).
**Claim under test.** 066:39: *"SHR-02 THE OTHER WAY: the kept share survives LIVE and still names the assignment it came from"*; SHR-02's cell (`docs/coverage.md:205`): *"a coordinator KEEPS one by id and it survives live, still naming the assignment it came from … 066:32–41 drive SHR-02 BOTH WAYS"*; ADR-0032 D3: *"which is what lets `unassign_task` revoke exactly its own — SHR-02 both ways"*; M1 header `:84–89`: *"UNASSIGN revokes EXACTLY this assignment's shares"*.
**What I found.** "This assignment" is implemented as "this task". After a keep, the kept row still says `created_by_assignment_of = task` (066:39 celebrates that it *"still names the assignment it came from"*), so from the next holder's point of view it is indistinguishable from a share that holder's assignment created. Any later `unassign_task(task)` without a keep list — which a manage-holder who is not a coordinator may call (066:45) — and any reassign (`assign_task` with a prior holder) revokes it. The runner already met this: case 52(b)'s comment reads *"The kept document share from (a) is revoked first so this assignment creates both shares afresh"* (`scripts/concurrency/run.mjs:3268–3269`) — the test author had to revoke the kept share by hand to get past exactly the collision this finding names.
**Failure scenario.** Probe A/B (`probe-r1.sql`) and P1 (`probe-r1b.sql`):
```
 A1 sarah assign t_der->lena path2                | share
 A2 sarah unassign KEEPING the doc share          | 1
 A3 kept share live? / lena reads d_med           | true/1
 A4 sarah assign t_der->marisol path1             | instruction
 A5 dan plain unassign of MARISOL: shares_revoked | 1
 A6 kept share live? / lena reads d_med           | false/1     ← Lena still reads d_med only because this fixture also gave her health:view
 A7 log: object_share_revoked entries naming Lena | 2
 B4 sarah REASSIGN t_plain->dan: shares_revoked   | 1
 B5 kept share (sh_doc2) live?                    | false
 P1c sarah unassign KEEPING marisol doc share     | 1
 P1d marisol reads d_med (kept)                   | 1
 P1e sarah assign t_der->dan plain                | plain
 P1f sarah unassign dan: shares_revoked           | 1
 P1g marisol reads d_med after (kept share gone?) | 0 kept_live=false
```
P1 is the caregiver case AC-TASK-7 and §4.6.3's table are about: Sarah (coordinator) keeps Marisol's discharge-summary share on unassign — the explicit decision the PRD reserves to a coordinator — hands the task to Dan, and unassigns Dan; Marisol's kept share is revoked as a side effect of an act about Dan, by a call that need not be a coordinator's (A5 is Dan's, at manage). The log entry (A7) names Lena as the target of a revocation "assignment_of t_der" for an assignment she no longer held. ### F-1 — BLOCKER — `hc.document_references` emits a row for every descendant the caller holds at `hidden`, disclosing the existence and count of objects that `tasks_select`, `hc.presence` and every sanctioned channel refuse her.
**Confidence.** High — mechanism read, scenario constructed, probe output observed on the live stack; the leak is pinned as the contract by two pgTAP cases.
**Where.** `supabase/migrations/20260829120004_record_reads.sql:266–273` — `hc.document_references`, the emit; `:272` the INNER `join lateral hc.object_label_at(v_ctx, r.otype, r.oid) x on true`; `:268–270` `case when x.level >= x.need then r.oid end … (x.level >= x.need)`. **Claim under test.** Header `:20–21`: "Each gets ONE definer, filtered per row through hc.visible_at, never wider than the RLS it stands in for." `:47–53`: "COUNTED, NEVER NAMED … `visible` is explicit … AC-PERM-10 falls out at the read: a share on the document lifts the document to view and lifts nothing derived from it." ADR-0032 D7: "counted-never-named through each object's OWN policy predicate". 069:411: "both are reported as existing and neither is named nor handed to her".

That sentence is now reachable only by a member holding **`log` or above on every domain in the taint**"; (:1023) "A count that reveals a hidden object's existence is a leak". AC-PERM-6 (:1038): "invisible — title, due date, **existence, and its contribution to every count** — … in any surface". TSD §3.5 (:1325): `hc.presence` "is the only route" to existence, "reachable only by a member holding `log` or above on every domain in the taint".

## Recorded dissents and observations

- **Dissent on the 6A precedent's transfer.** The header (`:5`, `:47`) and D7 lean on `hc.receipt_for`'s "counted, never named" discipline. The precedent is sound where it lives because its reader clears `view` on all five domains (`20260824120005:106–107`) and is the approver who wrote the destinations; every unnamed row there is deleted or unresolved lineage. Neither property holds for a document's `summary` reader or a caregiver with one named share. "Counted, never named" is the right discipline at `log`; at `hidden` it is the projection §7.6 struck. (F-1/F-2.)
- **The deleted-object line is drawn three ways**: `shares_for` → nothing (`resolve_object`), `document_references` → nothing (INNER join), `shares_for_member` → a counted row (LEFT join). `receipt_for` itself uses a LEFT join and counts a deleted destination — so the precedent is inconsistent on this too. (F-2.)

- **The invite-under-freeze reading** (`pending` while PRD §7.5 says "voided", tree FRZ-16 says "suspended") predates 7A; 069:15's `kind <> 'invite'` filter (`:382`) sidesteps rather than pins it. If PRD §4.1.6's pseudonymisation of a departed account ever rewrites `display_name`, the share lists will follow it and the log will not — consistent with the People list's `display_name_at_join` only by accident. Observation.
- **Timing.** `shares_for`'s nonexistent branch returns before `visible_at` is evaluated and the unmanageable branch after — a shape-identical, timing-different pair; PRD §4.3.6 and TSD Appendix A explicitly decline timing equivalence as a criterion, so this is recorded, not found.
